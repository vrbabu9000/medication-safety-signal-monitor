# Therapeutic class options — where is the impact?

_Stage: Define · Status: Draft for boss meeting 2026-10-13 · Data claims below are from general knowledge, **not yet checked against FAERS/recall data**._

## Ask the boss one question
> "Where do you want the impact to show: **patient harm inside the hospital**, a **story people already care about**, or **proof the AI works**?"

His answer picks the class:

| If he says… | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Patient harm in the hospital | **Anticoagulants** | Top cause of serious drug harm in hospitals; on the "high-alert medication" list (ISMP, the Institute for Safe Medication Practices) |
| A story people care about | **GLP-1 agonists** | Huge growth, in the news, recent label changes, compounded-product recalls |
| Proof the AI works | **GLP-1 agonists** or **fluoroquinolones** | Known label additions in recent years = a ready-made backtest ("would we have caught it?") |
| "You decide" | **GLP-1 agonists** (our default, G1) | Best balance of demo story, data volume and backtest |

## The candidates

| Class | Example drugs | Impact for the safety officer | Demo strength | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GLP-1 agonists** | semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy), tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound), liraglutide, dulaglutide | Growing inpatient issue: aspiration risk during anesthesia, holding doses before surgery | ⭐⭐⭐ News interest, label changes for backtest, compounded recalls | Small class (~6 ingredients). Reporting spikes after media coverage (stimulated reporting). Indication terms ("weight", "diabetes") show up as false reactions |
| **Anticoagulants** | warfarin, apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran, heparin, enoxaparin | Highest hospital relevance: bleeding events, dosing errors | ⭐⭐ Very credible to pharmacists | Labels are mature → fewer genuinely new signals. Bleeding dominates every analysis |
| **Fluoroquinolones** | ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin, moxifloxacin | Common hospital antibiotics; serious known risks | ⭐⭐ Clean backtest (several label safety updates over the last decade) | Old drugs, many generics → many labels per ingredient (DQ07) |
| **Immune checkpoint inhibitors** | pembrolizumab, nivolumab, atezolizumab | Oncology infusion centers; immune side effects still emerging | ⭐⭐ Real new signals | Cancer itself causes many reported events → confounding. Needs oncology knowledge |
| **"Top 100 inpatient drugs" formulary** | Mixed | Closest to her real job | ⭐ Broad but shallow | Breadth over depth. Against the brief's advice |

## 🩺 PV SME view
GLP-1s and fluoroquinolones give the cleanest "would we have caught it?" backtest. Anticoagulants are the most clinically serious but the least likely to show something *new*.

## 💊 Safety officer view
"Anticoagulants are what keep me up at night. GLP-1s are what my surgeons keep asking me about."
